How to write good queries

One topic per line = one reel. Each line should describe a single moment or subject as if you were tagging it. Use short noun phrases, not full sentences.

✓ Works well

match highlights
goal celebration
funny moment
transfer rumour
post-match interview

✗ Avoid

give me key highlights, predictions,
  coach, transfer market & some
  laughing moments.

show me everything funny

Tips: match your query language to the spoken language; if commentators say "best moments", that phrase will hit better than abstract synonyms.
